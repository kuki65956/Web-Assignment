Course Overview

This course focuses on the principles of cybersecurity, ethical hacking, network security, and data protection. You'll learn to defend against a variety of cyber attacks and secure systems in an ever-evolving digital world.

What You Will Learn

  • Fundamentals of Cybersecurity
  • Network Security and Protocols
  • Ethical Hacking and Penetration Testing
  • Data Encryption and Decryption
  • Security Risk Management
  • Incident Response and Recovery
