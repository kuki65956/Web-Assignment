Digital Marketing

Course Overview

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to digital marketing strategies, including social media marketing, SEO, email marketing, and paid ads. Learn to optimize online presence and grow a business in the digital age.

What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to Digital Marketing and its Importance
  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Techniques
  • Social Media Marketing (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn)
  • Pay-Per-Click (PPC) Advertising and Google Ads
  • Email Marketing and Automation
  • Content Marketing and Blogging
  • Analyzing Digital Marketing Campaigns with Analytics
