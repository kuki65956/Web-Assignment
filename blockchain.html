Blockchain Technology

Understand the power of decentralized systems and cryptocurrencies.

Course Overview

This course covers the fundamentals of blockchain technology, including its underlying architecture, cryptocurrencies, and smart contracts. You will learn how to design and develop blockchain-based solutions for various industries.

What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to Blockchain and its architecture
  • How cryptocurrencies work (Bitcoin, Ethereum, etc.)
  • Smart Contracts and DApps (Decentralized Applications)
  • Blockchain Security and Consensus Mechanisms
  • Blockchain Use Cases and Real-World Applications
  • Building Your Own Blockchain and Cryptocurrency
