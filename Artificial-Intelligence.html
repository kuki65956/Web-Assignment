Course Overview

This course covers the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence, from machine learning and deep learning to natural language processing and computer vision. You will learn how to implement AI models and algorithms in real-world scenarios.

What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and its applications
  • Machine Learning Algorithms (Supervised and Unsupervised Learning)
  • Deep Learning and Neural Networks
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP)
  • Computer Vision Techniques
  • AI Ethics and Bias
