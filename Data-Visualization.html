Data Visualization

Master the art of presenting complex data through compelling visuals.

Course Overview

This course will teach you how to convert raw data into meaningful visualizations. You will learn various techniques and tools to effectively present data, helping stakeholders understand complex information at a glance.

What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to Data Visualization and its Importance
  • Choosing the Right Type of Visualization for Your Data
  • Data Cleaning and Preparation for Visualization
  • Creating Interactive Dashboards with Tableau and Power BI
  • Data Visualization with Python (Matplotlib, Seaborn)
  • Design Principles for Effective Visualizations
  • Presenting Data to Different Audiences

Instructor
